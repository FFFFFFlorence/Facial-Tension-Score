TENSION WEIGHTS REFERENCE
==========================

The tension score uses six facial Action Units (AUs) from OpenFace. Each
weight controls how strongly that AU contributes to the visual tension score.

FACIAL ACTION UNITS
-------------------

AU04_r  0.228  Brow lowerer
AU09_r  0.192  Nose wrinkler
AU10_r  0.181  Upper lip raiser
AU20_r  0.146  Lip stretcher
AU15_r  0.131  Lip corner depressor
AU05_r  0.122  Upper lid raiser

The weights are data-derived from Cohen's d effect sizes calculated with the
project's facial-expression dataset. They are normalized coefficients, not
percentages. A higher weight means that the AU showed a stronger independent
ability to distinguish tense-associated expressions from baseline expressions.

SCORING FORMULA
---------------

AU score =
		(AU04_r * 0.228) +
		(AU09_r * 0.192) +
		(AU10_r * 0.181) +
		(AU20_r * 0.146) +
		(AU15_r * 0.131) +
		(AU05_r * 0.122)

Gaze variability is added to the facial channel:

Visual score = AU score + (gaze variance * 0.30)

The visual and voice channels are then combined:

Combined score = (visual score * 0.75) + (voice score * 0.25)

with the percentage of facial weight being 75% : and audio weight being 25%

The displayed tension value is relative to the target's baseline. During the
first 10 seconds of a session, the program collects baseline readings while
the target is expected to remain neutral. The baseline mean is subtracted from
later combined scores. The displayed score is smoothed using the most recent
10 readings.

INTERPRETATION NOTE
-------------------

These weights describe the relative contribution of model inputs. They do not
represent probabilities, percentages of tension, or proof of nervousness,
dishonesty, or intent. The resulting value is a heuristic pattern summary and
should be considered together with interview context and direct observation.
